FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before you book a Zaure service.

How do I book a service?
Each service has its own dedicated page — Zaure Findr, Zaure Clean, Zaure Move, and Zaure Delivery. Click the Book button on this page to go to that service's page, fill in a simple form, and confirm your booking.
Who are the professionals on Zaure Services?
Every cleaner, mover, delivery rider, and title lawyer on our roster has been individually vetted by Zaure — including background checks and a skills review. We only onboard professionals we'd use ourselves.
Which cities do you serve?
Zaure Findr works nationwide (land title verification has no city limits). Zaure Clean, Zaure Move, and Zaure Delivery are currently live in Kaduna and Abuja, with expansion to Lagos and Kano planned. For other cities, message us on WhatsApp and we'll see what we can arrange.
What types of items can Zaure Delivery carry?
Zaure Delivery is built for intra-city pickup and drop — documents, parcels, packages, small goods, and everyday items that need to move from one location to another inside the same city. For larger items or full home relocations, use Zaure Move instead.
How does payment work?
Each service page shows the full price upfront before you book. Payment is handled directly with Zaure — no commission, no hidden fees, no surprise charges after the job starts.
What if I'm not satisfied with the service?
Message us immediately on WhatsApp. We'll review the job and arrange a re-do or a fair refund as appropriate. Your satisfaction is what we care about most — that's the whole point of Zaure Services.
Do you take commission from sellers or providers?
No. Zaure has never taken commission from sellers on our marketplace, and we don't take it from service providers either. You pay a clear, upfront service fee — that's it.
Can I use Zaure Services outside Nigeria?
Yes — this is exactly what Zaure Findr was built for. If you're in the diaspora and want to buy land in Nigeria without being scammed, Findr handles verification, negotiation, and closing on your behalf. Cleaning, moving, and delivery require someone on the ground, but we can coordinate with family, tenants, or your agent.
