Questions, answered.

Which cities do you serve?
Zaure Findr works nationwide — land verification has no city limit. Cleaning, moving, and delivery are live in Kaduna and Abuja, expanding to Lagos and Kano soon. For other cities, message us on WhatsApp.
Who are the professionals?
Every Zaure pro is vetted: background-checked, skills-tested, and continuously reviewed. We don't onboard anyone we wouldn't use ourselves.
How does pricing work?
You see the full price before you book. No commission, no hidden fees, no surprise charges after the job starts. What you see is what you pay.
What if I'm not satisfied?
Message us on WhatsApp. We'll review the job and arrange a re-do or a fair refund. That's the standard — no exceptions.
Findr, Clean, Move, Delivery — which do I need?
Findr to buy land. Clean to clean a home or office. Move to relocate everything to a new address. Delivery to send a parcel or small item across your city.
