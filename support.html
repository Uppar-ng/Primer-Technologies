Common questions

Answers to what users ask us most

I paid a seller but the item never arrived
Report the seller immediately on WhatsApp with the chat screenshots, payment proof, and the listing link. We'll review the seller's account and may suspend it. Zaure is not a payment escrow — we can't recover money that was paid directly to a seller — but we can flag, suspend, and share account details with Nigerian authorities if there's clear evidence of fraud.
The item isn't what was described
Contact the seller first and ask for a resolution. If they refuse, report the listing to us on WhatsApp with photos showing the difference. We may remove the listing and take action against the seller's account. For future purchases, always inspect items in person before paying — that's the safest way to shop on Zaure.
How do I stay safe when buying?
Meet in a public place during daylight. Inspect the item carefully before paying. Don't pay in advance unless you're dealing with a verified business or Zaure Official Store. Bring someone with you for high-value purchases. Read our full Safety Tips.
How do I get verified?
Create a store profile and message us on WhatsApp to request verification. We check your phone number, email, and — for business verification — your CAC registration and physical address. Once verified, you'll see a badge on your profile and listings. Verification builds buyer trust and improves your listings' visibility.
How do I edit or delete a listing?
Go to Profile → My Listings, then use the Edit or Delete buttons on the listing. If you can't see the listing, it may have already been removed by our team for a policy issue — message us on WhatsApp to check.
Someone is trying to scam me as a seller
Common seller scams include fake payment alerts, overpayment schemes, and buyers claiming non-delivery after receiving goods. Always confirm payment has actually landed in your bank account (not just a screenshot). Report suspicious buyers on WhatsApp so we can flag their account.
What is MOQ and how does bulk pricing work?
MOQ stands for Minimum Order Quantity — the smallest number of units a seller will sell at wholesale pricing. On any listing with a "Buy in Bulk" option, you'll see a pricing table that shows the price per unit at different quantities. Use the calculator on the product page to see your exact total before messaging the seller.
How do I send a bulk quote request?
On the product page, set your quantity using the calculator, then tap "Send Quote on WhatsApp." The message is pre-filled with the product, quantity, unit price, tier, and total — so the seller receives a serious, ready-to-respond inquiry. For custom requirements, message them directly on WhatsApp and describe your needs.
How do I become a Zaure supplier (B2B)?
For bulk agro and business supply, message us on WhatsApp with your business name, products, MOQ, and coverage area. Our team reviews applications and may schedule a call or site visit for high-value suppliers. Verified suppliers receive priority placement in wholesale search results.
How do I book Zaure Clean?
Visit the Zaure Clean page and tap "Request a quote." Fill in your project details (location, size, handover date) and we'll respond on WhatsApp within a few hours with availability and pricing. For urgent bookings, message us directly on WhatsApp.
Can I reschedule or cancel a Zaure Clean booking?
Yes — message us on WhatsApp at least 48 hours before your scheduled date. Rescheduling is free if we have availability on your new date. Cancellations less than 24 hours before may forfeit the deposit, depending on the size of the job.
What if I'm not happy with a Zaure Clean job?
Contact us within 24 hours with photos of any issue. We'll return to re-clean the affected areas free of charge. If you're still not satisfied, we'll refund you in full. This is our standard for every Zaure Clean job — no questions asked.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This removes your profile, listings, and favorites permanently. If you want us to delete any server-side records too, message us on WhatsApp with your registered phone number. See our Privacy Policy for more on how we handle your data.
How do I update my phone number or state?
Go to Profile → tap "Edit Profile" → update your details → tap "Save Changes." Your phone number is used for verification and buyer contact — only update it if you're sure the new number is reliable.
How do favorites work?
Tap the heart icon on any product, service, or hotspot to save it to your favorites. Your favorites are stored on your account and visible on the Favorites page. You need to be logged in to use favorites.
