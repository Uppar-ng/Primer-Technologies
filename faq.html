Buying on Zaure

How do I contact a seller?

Open any product page and tap the "Message on WhatsApp" button. WhatsApp opens with a message pre-filled with the product name and price, so the seller knows exactly what you're asking about.

Zaure doesn't have its own messaging system — every conversation happens on WhatsApp because that's where Nigerian commerce already lives.

Is Zaure free to buy on?

Yes — 100% free for buyers. Zaure charges no commission, no buyer fees, and no hidden charges. You only pay the seller for the item you buy.

How do I save items to favorites?

Tap the heart icon on any product, service, or hotspot. You need to be signed in. Your saved items show on the Favorites page, and the number next to the heart in the header shows your total.

How do I search for items near me?

Your recommendations are automatically sorted by your state (from your profile), so you see local sellers first. You can also use the search bar and filter by category on the Browse page.

What if the item isn't what was described?

Contact the seller first on WhatsApp and ask for a resolution. If they refuse, report the listing to us with photos showing the difference — we may remove the listing and take action on the seller's account.

Zaure can't force a refund because we're not a payment processor. But we do act on reports of sellers who misrepresent their items.
Can I get delivery?

Zaure doesn't operate its own delivery. Delivery is arranged directly between you and the seller — some deliver within their city, others require pickup. Message them on WhatsApp to confirm before you commit.

Selling on Zaure

How do I post a listing?

Tap the "+" button in the bottom navigation, or tap "Post a listing" on the homepage. Fill in the title, description, price, category, and upload photos — your listing goes live immediately. No review queue, no waiting.

Is posting free?

Yes — 100% free. Unlimited listings. No commission on sales. No hidden fees. You keep everything you earn.

How many listings can I post?

Unlimited. There's no cap on listings, whether you're an individual or a business. Post as many as you want.

How long until my listing goes live?

Immediately. As soon as you tap "Publish," your listing is on the marketplace and visible to buyers in your category. No review delay.

How do I edit or delete a listing?

Go to Profile → My Listings. Each listing has an Edit button and a Delete button. Edits go live immediately; deletes are permanent.

Can I boost my listing?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with the listing link. Boosted listings appear at the top of category pages and get featured in homepage ad slots — typically 5–10x more views.

How do I protect myself from buyer scams?

Common tricks to watch for:

  • Fake payment screenshots. Always confirm the money landed in your bank app — not a screenshot.
  • Overpayment scam. Buyer "accidentally" sends too much and asks for a refund. Their original transfer was fake.
  • "My agent will collect." Get written confirmation before releasing goods.

Full patterns on the Safety Tips page.

Wholesale & B2B

What is MOQ?

Minimum Order Quantity — the smallest number of units a seller will sell at wholesale pricing. If MOQ is 50, you either buy 50+ at the wholesale rate, or pay retail for smaller quantities.

How does tier pricing work?

Wholesale prices come in tiers — the more you buy, the lower the price per unit. Example:

  • 10–49 units: ₦25,000 each
  • 50–99 units: ₦22,000 each
  • 100+ units: ₦20,000 each

The calculator on each product page shows your exact total at any quantity.

How do I send a bulk quote request?

On the product page, set your quantity in the "Buy in Bulk" calculator, then tap "Send Quote on WhatsApp". The seller receives a message with the product, quantity, unit price, and total — ready to respond to.

How do I become a verified supplier?

Message us on WhatsApp with your business name, CAC number, products, and MOQ. For high-value suppliers (agro, industrial), we may arrange a call or site visit. Verified suppliers get priority placement and buyer trust.

How do I stay safe in B2B transactions?

Bulk orders involve serious money — the checks should match:

  • Visit the warehouse before committing
  • Check CAC registration
  • Order a sample first
  • Use a written agreement
  • Never pay 100% upfront — 30–50% deposit is standard

Full checklist on the Safety Tips page.

Hotspots

What are Hotspots?

A curated guide to restaurants, cafes, bars, beaches, parks, and landmarks across Nigeria. Browse by category, search by name, or sort by nearest. Each has photos, hours (with real-time open/closed), and a location map.

How does "nearest to me" work?

If you allow browser geolocation, we sort hotspots by distance from you. The distance is calculated in your browser — your coordinates aren't stored on our servers. If you decline, hotspots are shown newest-first.

Are the hours accurate?

We show real-time Open/Closed badges based on each hotspot's stated hours and Lagos time, updating automatically every minute. But hours can change on holidays — for a place you're travelling to, call ahead to confirm.

Can I book through Zaure?

No — Hotspots is a discovery guide only. We don't own, operate, or endorse any venue. Bookings and payments are between you and the venue directly.

Account

How do I create an account?

Tap the avatar in the top-right of any page, or the Profile tab at the bottom. Enter your name, phone number, and state — takes under a minute. No email verification needed.

How do I edit my profile?

Go to Profile → Edit Profile. Update name, phone, email, state, or bio, then tap Save Changes.

How do I change my profile picture?

On your profile page, tap the camera icon on your avatar. Pick a photo from your device. Your photo is stored locally — it won't sync across devices.

How do I change the language or dark mode?

Both are in Profile → Settings. Zaure supports English, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, and Arabic. Language preference applies site-wide.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Confirm twice. This permanently removes your profile, listings, and favorites. For server-side deletion beyond what the app removes, message us on WhatsApp.

I lost my data after clearing my browser. Can I get it back?

Most Zaure data (favorites, listings, avatar, theme) is stored in your browser's localStorage — not on our servers. If you clear browser data, it's gone.

This is a known limitation we're working on. For now, keep a backup of any listings you care about in a note or photo folder.

Safety

What does the verified badge mean?

We've checked certain details — usually phone, email, and for businesses, CAC registration and address. It does not guarantee the seller will never scam you, or that the item is exactly as described.

Verification is one signal, not a guarantee. Always inspect, meet, and check before paying — verified or not.
What should I do if I encounter a scam?

Report it immediately on WhatsApp with screenshots of the conversation and the listing link. We act on all reports within 24 hours — suspend the account, and share details with authorities if you want to file a police report.

Where should I meet buyers or sellers?

Public, well-lit places during daylight — inside a shopping mall, at a bank, at a police station, or in a busy market. Never at your home or their home. Bring someone with you for high-value transactions.

Should I pay in advance?

Never to strangers. No deposits, no booking fees, no exceptions. Pay after inspecting the item, in person, at the moment of handover. If a seller insists on advance payment, walk away — there's always another listing.

Can I recover money I lost to a scam?

Zaure can't recover money paid directly to a seller — we're not a payment processor. Your options:

  • Report to us — we suspend the seller's account
  • Report to your bank — some can attempt a reversal for very recent transfers
  • Report to police or EFCC — for significant amounts
