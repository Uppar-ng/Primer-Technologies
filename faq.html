Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about Zaure

Buying on Zaure

How do I contact a seller?
Open the product listing and click the "Call Seller" button to call them directly, or click "WhatsApp" to chat with them on WhatsApp. No middlemen involved.
Is it safe to buy on Zaure?
Yes! We verify sellers and provide safety guidelines. Always meet in public places, inspect items before purchase, avoid advance payments, and trust your instincts.
How do I save items to favorites?
Click the heart icon on any listing to save it to your favorites. You must be signed in to use this feature. Your favorites are saved in your profile.
How do I search for items near me?
Our platform uses location-based recommendations. Sign up with your state, and you'll see items from your area first. You can also use the search bar to find specific items.
Do I need to pay to buy on Zaure?
No! Zaure is 100% free for buyers. You only pay the seller for the item you're purchasing. We charge no commission or hidden fees.

Account

How do I create an account?
Click the avatar icon in the top navigation and fill out the signup form with your name, phone number, and state. It takes less than a minute.
How do I edit my profile?
Go to your Profile page, click "Edit Profile," make your changes, and click "Save Changes." You can update your name, phone, email, state, and bio.
How do I change my profile picture?
Go to your Profile page and click the camera icon on your avatar. Select an image from your device to upload.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account. This action is permanent and will remove all your data.

Safety

How does Zaure verify sellers?
We verify sellers through phone number confirmation and track their activity. Verified sellers receive a badge on their listings and store pages.
What should I do if I encounter a scam?
Report it immediately via WhatsApp: +2349042339411. Our team reviews all reports within 24 hours and takes appropriate action.
Where should I meet buyers/sellers?
Always meet in public, well-lit places like shopping malls, police stations, or busy markets. Never invite strangers to your home.
Should I pay in advance?
No! Never pay for items you haven't seen. Be cautious of sellers who demand advance payments or deposits before delivery.
