What Zaure Move handles

Zaure Move is Zaure's in-house moving service. We handle small and medium moves across Kaduna — students, families, small offices, shops, and landlords.

Packing, loading, transport, unloading, and placement. Every move is led by a Zaure Move supervisor and supported by our vetted network of vehicle owners — one point of contact, one schedule, one crew from your door to the new one.

What we handle on moving day

Everything from the first box packed to the last box placed. You point, we move.

Packing & Wrapping

Boxes, bubble wrap, and furniture covers — supplied and packed by our crew. Every item labelled and secured.

Loading & Unloading

Trained crew loads the vehicle safely and unloads it at the destination. Heavy items, fragile items, and awkward furniture handled right.

Transport Coordination

We arrange and manage the right vehicle for your move — briefed, scheduled, and supervised by our team from pickup to delivery.

Move Supervision

A Zaure Move supervisor is on site from start to finish — directing the crew, keeping the schedule, and answering anything as it comes up.

What we don't do

Zaure Move handles small and medium moves only. These jobs are outside our scope.

Outside our scope

  • Large house moves (5+ bedrooms) — outside our scope.
  • Interstate or long-distance moves — we operate within Kaduna and nearby towns.
  • Heavy industrial equipment — machinery, generators above 20KVA, and factory relocations need heavy logistics partners.
  • Fleet ownership — we coordinate transport through our vetted network of vehicle owners.
