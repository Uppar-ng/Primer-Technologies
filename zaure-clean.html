Zaure Clean

We handle the final clean on construction and renovation projects — from debris removal to handover-ready finishes. Trusted by contractors, engineers, and property developers across Northern Nigeria.

Message on WhatsApp

Built for the people who finish buildings

Zaure Clean is a specialist post-construction cleaning sub-contractor. We take on the last job on the site — after the plaster has dried, the tiles are laid, and the electrical work is done. When your client walks in for the final inspection, we've already been through.

Contractors call us because we work to their schedule, not the other way around. Same-week mobilisation, documented completion, and a clean-site report after every job.

What we do on site

Full post-construction scope — from rough debris to final polish, plus pest control, weed control, and carpet cleaning.

Rough Clean

Debris removal, packaging disposal, and site clearing after trades have finished.

Dust Extraction

Full extraction from walls, ceilings, vents, and every surface dust settles on.

Deep Surface Clean

Floors, tiles, skirting, doors, frames, windows — cleaned to spec, room by room.

Glass & Frame Polish

Interior and exterior glass, aluminium frames, and window tracks — no streaks.

Paint & Cement Removal

Careful removal of splatter and residue without damaging finished surfaces.

Pest Control

Pre-handover fumigation and treatment — insects, rodents, and nesting cleared before you open the doors.

Weed Control

Compound clearing, overgrowth removal, and site-edge weeding for residential and commercial plots.

Carpet Cleaning

Deep steam extraction for carpets and rugs — stains, odours, and embedded dirt removed.

Final Handover Clean

Handover-ready finish — the last pass before your client's walk-through.

Before we arrive

A few things that make the job faster and handover smoother.

Ensure the site is ready for cleaning — no active trades working
Have keys or site access arranged for the crew
Confirm water and electricity are available on site
Remove any equipment, tools, or materials that aren't staying
Have your client's inspection contact ready for the walk-through
