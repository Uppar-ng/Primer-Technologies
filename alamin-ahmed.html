Alamin Ahmed

Alamin Ahmed founded Zaure Technologies in 2025. Zaure's mission is to make commerce safe, free, and accessible for every Nigerian. Zaure is a platform where buyers find the best products at the best prices, with verified sellers, fast delivery on thousands of items across all 36 states of Nigeria, and dedicated storefronts for trusted businesses. Ahmed is also the driving force behind Fulfillment by Zaure, which handles storage, packing, and delivery for the platform's most trusted sellers, and Zaure's trust-first checkout system, which protects both buyers and sellers in every transaction. Zaure is pursuing the long-term goal of becoming Africa's most trusted online marketplace. Ahmed founded the company at 23 after a gradual realization that Nigerian commerce had a trust problem, not a supply problem. He was born and raised in Kaduna, Nigeria, where he still lives and works.
