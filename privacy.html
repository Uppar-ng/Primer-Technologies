01 Who We Are

Zaure is operated by Zaure Technologies, a Nigerian company. We run the Zaure marketplace, wholesale tier, Zaure Services (including Zaure Clean), Zaure Official Store, the El brand, Hotspots, and Find a Pro.

For the purposes of Nigeria's Data Protection Regulation (NDPR, 2019) and the Nigeria Data Protection Act (2023), Zaure Technologies is the data controller for the personal information described in this policy.

02 Scope of This Policy

This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and your rights over it. It applies to everyone who uses Zaure — buyers, sellers, wholesale buyers, service customers, and visitors.

It does not cover:

  • WhatsApp conversations between users (governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy)
  • Third-party websites you visit through links on Zaure
  • Any platform we don't own or operate

03 Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: Full name, phone number, email address, and state when you sign up
  • Profile information: Bio, profile picture, business details (if you register as a business seller)
  • Listing information: Title, description, price, photos, category, location of items you sell
  • Service bookings: For Zaure Clean — property location, size, preferred dates, contact details
  • Communications: Messages you send us via email, WhatsApp, or the contact form
  • Verification documents: If you apply for verification — CAC registration, business address, ID (see Section 11)

Information collected automatically

  • Device & browser data: Browser type, device type, operating system, screen size
  • Usage data: Pages visited, links clicked, time spent, search queries (aggregated and anonymized)
  • Approximate location: Only if you allow browser geolocation — used to show nearby hotspots and products (see Section 10)
  • IP address: For security and analytics, stored in abbreviated form

Information we do NOT collect

  • Payment card details (Zaure doesn't process marketplace payments)
  • WhatsApp conversation content (we don't have access to it)
  • Contacts from your phone
  • Photos from your camera roll (only photos you explicitly upload)

06 Legal Basis for Processing

Under the NDPR and Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, we process your data on these legal grounds:

  • Contract — to provide the Zaure platform you've signed up for
  • Legitimate interest — to prevent fraud, improve security, and grow the platform
  • Consent — for geolocation, marketing emails, and non-essential cookies (you can withdraw at any time)
  • Legal obligation — to comply with tax, fraud-prevention, or court orders

07 Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share only where necessary:

Who we share with

  • Other Zaure users — your public profile, listings, and contact number (as you've entered it) are visible to other users
  • Service providers — analytics (Google Analytics), form handling (Formspree), hosting (Netlify/Vercel), all bound by data processing agreements
  • Verification partners — when you request verification, we share your submitted documents with partners who confirm them (only if you opt in)
  • Law enforcement — only in response to a lawful warrant or court order, and only after internal review
  • Successor entity — if Zaure is acquired or merged, your data may transfer to the new entity under the same protections

What we never share

  • Your phone number with anyone who isn't directly transacting with you
  • Your email with third-party marketers
  • Your verification documents with anyone except our verification partner
  • Your IP address or device fingerprint with anyone

08 WhatsApp & Third Parties

Zaure does not have a native messaging system. Every conversation between buyers and sellers happens on WhatsApp.

Important: When you message a seller on WhatsApp, that conversation is between you and them. Zaure cannot see it, read it, record it, or access it in any way. WhatsApp's own privacy policy and end-to-end encryption govern that conversation.

What we can see on our side:

  • When you click a WhatsApp button on Zaure (we log that a click happened, but not the content of the message you sent)
  • Your public profile information visible on your Zaure listing
  • Any screenshots or reports you voluntarily send us about a conversation

We recommend reading WhatsApp's Privacy Policy so you understand how they handle your data.

09 Cookies & Local Storage

Zaure uses browser localStorage more than traditional cookies. Here's what we store on your device:

KeyWhat it storesWhere
zaure_user Your account details (name, phone, state, bio) Your device only
zaure_favorites Product and service IDs you've saved Your device only
zaure_hotspot_favorites Hotspots you've saved Your device only
zaure_theme Light or dark mode preference Your device only

Important: Most of this data lives only on your device — it doesn't reach our servers until you perform an action that requires it (like posting a listing that gets synced). Clearing your browser data will remove all of this from your device.

We also use a small number of traditional cookies from third-party services:

  • Analytics cookies — from Google Analytics, to understand aggregate traffic patterns
  • No advertising cookies — we don't run third-party ads or retargeting

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may affect platform functionality.

10 Analytics & Tracking

We use Google Analytics to understand how people use Zaure — which pages are popular, where users get stuck, and where to focus improvement.

  • Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized — we don't see "John from Kaduna clicked this"
  • We anonymize IP addresses before they reach Google
  • We do not use Google Analytics advertising features (remarketing, interest reports)
  • We do not use Facebook Pixel or similar behavioral tracking

Geolocation

We use your browser's geolocation only if you explicitly allow it — the browser shows a permission prompt and you choose. It's used to:

  • Sort hotspots by distance from you
  • Show products from sellers near you

Your coordinates are used in the browser to sort the results. They are not stored on our servers. You can revoke geolocation permission at any time from your browser settings.

11 Verification Data

When you apply for verification — as an individual seller or a business — we may collect:

  • Phone number verification — an OTP sent to your number
  • Email verification — a link sent to your email
  • Business verification — CAC registration number, business address, bank account confirmation
  • Identity verification — a government ID (only for high-tier verification, and only if you opt in)
What happens with verification data: It's stored securely, encrypted at rest, and used only to confirm your identity and business. It is never shown publicly. Only your verified badge appears on your profile — not the underlying documents.

You can request deletion of verification documents at any time by contacting us. If deleted, your verified badge will be removed and you'll need to re-verify to regain it.

12 Business & Wholesale Data

If you register as a business seller or engage in wholesale (B2B) transactions, we may additionally collect:

  • Business name, CAC number, business address
  • Product catalogs, MOQ, pricing tiers

Business information is used to verify your business, display your profile on the marketplace, and help wholesale buyers find you. Personal contact details (phone, email) are shown only to users who interact with you.

15 Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

  • HTTPS encryption on all pages — your connection to Zaure is encrypted
  • Encrypted storage for sensitive data like verification documents
  • Access controls — only staff who need your data can access it
  • Regular backups with encryption
  • Vendor due diligence — we work only with service providers who meet our security standards
But: No method of internet transmission is 100% secure. We can't guarantee that a determined attacker will never breach our systems. If we become aware of a data breach affecting you, we will notify you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission promptly, as required by law.

What you can do to help:

  • Use a strong password and don't share it
  • Log out from shared devices
  • Never send passwords or OTPs to anyone claiming to be from Zaure

16 Your Rights

Under NDPR and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access — request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correction — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to deletion — ask us to delete your data (with some exceptions for legal obligations)
  • Right to restrict processing — ask us to stop using your data for certain purposes
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Right to withdraw consent — withdraw consent for marketing or geolocation at any time

To exercise any of these rights, message us on WhatsApp or email hello@zauretech.xyz. We'll respond within 30 days.

17 Account Deletion & Data Erasure

You can delete your account any time from Profile → Settings → Delete Account. When you do:

  • Your profile, listings, and favorites are immediately removed from public view
  • Your data is fully deleted from our servers within 30 days (except where retention is required by law)
  • Your data is removed from backups within 90 days
  • Your verified badge is removed and cannot be restored without re-verification
Some data we cannot delete immediately: fraud reports, dispute records, or data we're legally required to retain (e.g., for tax or law enforcement). We'll tell you what we're keeping and why if you request deletion.

If you need us to delete server-side records faster than 30 days, message us on WhatsApp with your registered phone number.

19 International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers are based outside Nigeria — for example, Google Analytics (United States), Netlify or Vercel (United States), and WhatsApp (Meta, United States). When your data is transferred outside Nigeria, we ensure:

  • The receiving country has adequate data protection standards, OR
  • Our contracts with those providers include standard data protection clauses
  • The transfer is necessary for providing you the service

Your verification documents are never transferred outside Nigeria unless you specifically opt in for international verification partners.
