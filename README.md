# Zaure

**Buy with Confidence.**

Zaure is a Nigerian marketplace that connects buyers, sellers, service
providers, and local businesses in one trusted space. From phones and
furniture to post-construction cleaning and bulk agro commodities — Zaure
is built to make local commerce feel safe, direct, and honest.

No commission. No middlemen. Just trade.

---

## What Zaure Does

### 🛒 A marketplace for everything local
Buy and sell products across categories — electronics, fashion, furniture,
vehicles, real estate, home & kitchen, beauty & health, food & drinks.
Free to list, unlimited listings, no commission on any sale.

### 📦 Wholesale & B2B
Sellers choose how they sell — retail (single), wholesale (bulk), or both.
Buyers see tiered pricing, minimum order quantities, and a live bulk price
calculator before they message. Perfect for shops, resellers, contractors,
and exporters.

### 🧹 Zaure Clean — house service
Our in-house post-construction cleaning service — rough clean, dust
extraction, deep surface clean, glass & frame polish, paint & cement
removal, pest control, weed control, carpet cleaning, and final handover.
Trusted by contractors, engineers, and property developers across
Northern Nigeria.

### 🏪 Zaure Official Store & El
Products sold directly by Zaure, plus **El** — our house lifestyle brand
for shoe polish, shampoo, and everyday goods. Verified, curated, and
backed by us.

### 🔥 Hotspots
A curated guide to restaurants, cafes, bars, beaches, parks, landmarks,
and cultural sites across Nigeria. Real-time open/closed status,
location-aware sorting, and contact details — so you know what's near
and what's open before you go.

### 🛠 Find a Pro
A directory of local service professionals — plumbers, electricians,
carpenters, cleaners, technicians. Browse by category, view profiles,
and message them directly.

### 💬 Direct Contact
Every conversation happens on WhatsApp — because that's where Nigerian
commerce already lives. No second messaging app to check. No cold
inbox. Just a direct line to the seller, buyer, or pro.

### 🛡 Trust First
Verified sellers, business verification (CAC, address, bank), safety
guides, fraud reporting, and documented terms. We can't remove every
scam, but we can make trust visible and enforcement real.

### 🌍 Bilingual by design
English, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Fulani, and Arabic — language switching
site-wide from the user profile. Built for Nigeria's diversity from
day one.
