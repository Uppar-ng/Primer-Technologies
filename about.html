About Zaure

Buy with Confidence

Zaure is Nigeria's most trusted online marketplace — a place where buyers, sellers, and service providers meet without middlemen, without commission, and without fear of being scammed.

Our Story

Zaure was born from a simple observation: Nigerian commerce doesn't have a supply problem — it has a trust problem.

Nigerians buy and sell every day. Markets are full. WhatsApp groups overflow with listings. Facebook Marketplace and Jiji buzz with activity. But across every platform, the same complaint echoes: "How do I know this seller is real?"

In 2025, our founder decided to fix that. Not with another ad platform. Not with another commission-based marketplace. But with something that treats trust as the foundation, not the afterthought.

That's Zaure — a marketplace where every seller is verifiable, every listing can be trusted, and every transaction can happen with confidence. No middlemen. No hidden fees. Just real people, trading honestly.

Trust First. Always.

Every feature we build starts with the same question: "Does this make trading safer?" If it doesn't, we don't ship it.

What We Stand For

No Commission

We don't take a cut from your sale. What you earn is what you keep. Sellers list for free, and always will.

Verification

Sellers and service providers verify their identity. Buyers know exactly who they're dealing with before making contact.

Direct Contact

No chatbots, no waiting. Buyers reach sellers instantly on WhatsApp or call — the same way Nigerians already trade.

Local First

Zaure connects you to sellers and pros in your city, not across the country. Buy close. Support local.

Safety by Default

Every listing goes through review. Bad actors are removed quickly. Reporting a scam takes one tap.

Both Sides Win

Buyers get better prices. Sellers keep more of what they earn. When both sides win, the platform grows itself.
