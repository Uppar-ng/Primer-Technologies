Buying on Zaure

How do I contact a seller?
Open the product listing and click the "Call Seller" or "WhatsApp" button to contact the seller directly.
Is it safe to buy on Zaure?
Yes! We verify sellers and provide safety tips. Always meet in public places, inspect items before purchase, and avoid advance payments.
How do I save items to favorites?
Click the heart icon on any listing to save it to your favorites. You must be signed in to use this feature.

Selling on Zaure

How do I post an ad?
Click the "+" button in the bottom navigation, or go to the Post page and fill out the Google Form to submit your listing.
Is posting free?
Yes! Posting on Zaure is 100% free. No commission, no hidden fees.

Account Help

How do I create an account?
Click the avatar icon in the header and fill out the signup form with your name, phone number, and state.
How do I edit my profile?
Go to your Profile page, click "Edit Profile," make your changes, and click "Save Changes."
How do I delete my account?
Go to Profile → Settings → Account and click "Delete Account." This action is permanent.
