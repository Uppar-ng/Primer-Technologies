Getting Started

New to Zaure? Start here.

What is Zaure?

Zaure is Nigeria's trusted online marketplace. You can use it to buy and sell items locally, order in bulk from verified suppliers, book professional services like Zaure Clean, and discover places to eat, relax, and hang out through Hotspots.

Zaure is not a payment processor, escrow service, or delivery company. We connect buyers and sellers — the transaction happens directly between you and the other party.

Is Zaure free to use?

Yes, completely free — for both buyers and sellers. There's no commission on any marketplace transaction, no listing fee, and no hidden charges.

Zaure makes money through its own services (like Zaure Clean), the Zaure Official Store, and the El brand. Third-party sellers on the marketplace keep 100% of what they sell.

How do I create an account?

Tap the avatar icon in the top-right of any page, or the Profile tab at the bottom. Fill in your name, phone number, and state, then tap Sign Up. That's it — no email verification needed to start browsing and saving favorites.

Do I need an account to browse?

No. You can browse all products, categories, hotspots, services, and stores without an account. You only need one to:

  • Save items to favorites
  • Post a listing
  • Book a Zaure service
  • Appear as a verified seller

Buying on Zaure

How to find, contact, and buy from sellers safely.

How do I contact a seller?

Open any product page and tap the "Message on WhatsApp" button. This opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message that includes the product name and price — so the seller knows exactly what you're asking about.

Zaure doesn't have its own messaging system. Every conversation happens on WhatsApp, because that's where Nigerian commerce already lives.

How do I buy in bulk (wholesale)?

Products that offer wholesale will show a "Buy in Bulk" card on the product page. Use the quantity calculator to see your exact total, then tap "Send Quote on WhatsApp" — the message goes to the seller with the product, quantity, unit price, and total already filled in.

The seller will respond with availability, lead time, and final pricing. For full details, see the Wholesale & B2B section below.

Is it safe to buy on Zaure?

Zaure provides the tools (verified badges, safety tips, reporting), but the actual transaction is between you and the seller. We can't recover money you've paid directly to a seller.

Always: inspect the item in person before paying. Never pay in advance to strangers. Meet in a public place.

Read the full Safety Tips page for detailed guidance on safe buying.

How do I save items to favorites?

Tap the heart icon on any product, service, or hotspot. You need to be signed in. Your saved items appear on the Favorites page, and the number on the heart in the header shows your total.

What if the item isn't what was described?

Contact the seller first on WhatsApp and ask for a resolution. If they refuse, report the listing to us with photos showing the difference — we may remove the listing and take action on the seller's account.

Zaure can't force a refund because we're not a payment processor. But we can and do act on reports of sellers who repeatedly misrepresent their items.

Can I get delivery?

Zaure doesn't operate its own delivery service. Delivery is arranged directly between you and the seller. Some sellers offer delivery within their city, others require pickup. Message them on WhatsApp to confirm before you commit to buying.

For Zaure Clean and other Zaure-operated services, our staff come to your location as part of the booking.

Selling on Zaure

How to list your items, get seen, and sell safely.

How do I post a listing?

Tap the "+" button in the bottom navigation, or tap "Post a listing" on the homepage. Fill in the item title, description, price, category, and upload photos.

Photos matter. Listings with clear photos get 3–5x more messages than those without. Take photos in good lighting from multiple angles.

Is posting free?

Yes — 100% free. No listing fee, no commission on sales, no hidden charges. You keep everything you earn.

How do I price my item?

Look at similar items already listed on Zaure in your category. Price competitively but honestly — you can always lower the price later, but starting too high often means nobody messages you at all.

For items in good condition, a common starting point is 60–75% of the original price if the item is used but well-kept.

How do I edit or delete a listing?

Go to Profile → My Listings. Each listing has an Edit button and a Delete button. Edits go live immediately; deletes are permanent.

If you can't find a listing you posted, message us on WhatsApp — it may have been removed by our team for a policy issue.

How do I boost my listing?

Message us on WhatsApp at +234 904 233 9411 with the listing link. Boosted listings appear at the top of category pages and get featured in the homepage ad slots — typically 5–10x more views.

Pricing depends on duration and category. We'll send you a quote.

How do I protect myself from buyer scams?

Common buyer tricks:

  • Fake payment screenshots. Always confirm the money landed in your account via your bank app — not a screenshot, not an SMS.
  • Overpayment scam. Buyer sends "too much" and asks for a refund. Their original transfer was fake.
  • "My agent will collect." Get written confirmation from the buyer that their agent collected the item, or don't release the goods.

Read the full Safety Tips for detailed patterns.

Wholesale & B2B

For shops, resellers, contractors, and businesses buying in bulk.

What is MOQ?

MOQ stands for Minimum Order Quantity — the smallest number of units a seller will sell at wholesale pricing. If a product's MOQ is 50, you can't buy 30 at the wholesale price — you either buy 50+ or pay the retail price for a smaller quantity.

MOQ is set by each seller individually. Different sellers of the same product may have different MOQs.

How does tier pricing work?

Wholesale prices usually come in tiers — the more you buy, the lower the price per unit. For example:

  • 10–49 units: ₦25,000 each
  • 50–99 units: ₦22,000 each
  • 100+ units: ₦20,000 each

On the product page, use the quantity calculator to see your exact total at any quantity before you message the seller.

How do I send a wholesale quote request?

On the product page, scroll to the "Buy in Bulk" card, set your quantity, and tap "Send Quote on WhatsApp". The message to the seller includes:

  • Product name
  • Quantity you want
  • Unit price for that tier
  • Total price

The seller responds with availability, lead time, and any additional terms. Most B2B negotiations continue on WhatsApp from there.

How do I become a verified supplier?

Message us on WhatsApp with:

  • Business name and CAC registration number
  • Products you supply and your MOQ
  • Coverage area / shipping regions
  • Website or social media (if any)

For high-value suppliers (agro commodities, industrial materials), we may arrange a call or site visit before verifying. Verified suppliers receive priority placement and buyer trust.

How do I protect myself in B2B transactions?

Bulk orders involve much more money than single purchases — the due diligence should be proportionally bigger:

  • Visit the warehouse before committing. No address, no deal.
  • Check CAC registration. Real businesses have it.
  • Order a sample first. One unit of 100 should match the whole order.
  • Use a written agreement — even a simple one on letterhead with delivery terms, quality standard, and dispute resolution.
  • Never pay 100% upfront on first-time orders. 30–50% deposit, balance on inspection is standard.
  • Bank transfer only to a business account matching the company name.

Zaure Clean

Post-construction cleaning, pest control, weed control, and carpet cleaning.

What is Zaure Clean?

Zaure Clean is Zaure's in-house post-construction cleaning service. We specialise in final-clean work for contractors, engineers, and property developers — the last job on a site before handover.

Our scope covers rough clean, dust extraction, deep surface clean, glass & frame polish, paint & cement removal, pest control, weed control, carpet cleaning, and final handover clean.

Full details on the Zaure Clean page.

How do I book Zaure Clean?

Visit zaure-clean.html and tap "Request a quote". Fill in the form with project details (location, size, handover date) — we respond on WhatsApp within a few hours with availability and pricing.

For urgent bookings, message us directly on WhatsApp.

What does a Zaure Clean booking include?

Every job is scoped based on the site visit or details you provide. The quote you receive will specify exactly what's included — floors, surfaces, glass, and any specialist work like carpet extraction or fumigation.

We arrive with all equipment and products needed for the job. You don't need to supply anything.

How do I pay for a Zaure Clean job?

Standard terms: 50% deposit to confirm the booking, 50% balance on completion. Bank transfer only — we don't collect cash on-site. You'll receive a receipt after every job.

All payments go through official Zaure channels. If anyone claiming to be from Zaure Clean asks you to pay cash to them personally, refuse and report it immediately.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp at least 48 hours before your scheduled date. Rescheduling is free if we have availability on your new date. Cancellations less than 24 hours before may forfeit the deposit.

What if I'm not happy with the job?

Contact us within 24 hours with photos of any issue. We'll return to re-clean the affected areas free of charge. If you're still not satisfied, we'll refund you in full.

This is our standard: no arguments, no back-and-forth. If it's not right, we make it right or refund you.

Hotspots

A discovery guide to places across Nigeria.

What are Hotspots?

Hotspots is a curated guide to restaurants, cafes, bars, beaches, parks, landmarks, and cultural sites across Nigeria. Browse by category, search by name, or sort by nearest to your location.

Each hotspot has photos, hours (with real-time open/closed status), a location map, and contact details.

How does "nearest to me" work?

If you allow browser geolocation, we sort hotspots by distance from your current location. The distance is calculated in your browser — your coordinates aren't sent to our servers.

If you don't allow geolocation, hotspots are shown by newest-first instead.

Are the hours accurate?

We show real-time "Open" or "Closed" badges based on each hotspot's stated hours and Nigeria (Lagos) time. This updates automatically every minute.

However — hours can change on holidays or without notice. For a place you're travelling to, we recommend calling ahead to confirm.

Is Zaure involved in bookings at hotspots?

No. Hotspots is a discovery guide only. We don't own, operate, or endorse any venue. Bookings, payments, and visits are entirely between you and the venue.

If a hotspot has moved or closed, report it on WhatsApp and we'll update the listing.

Find a Pro

Connect with local service professionals.

What is Find a Pro?

Find a Pro is a directory of local service professionals — plumbers, electricians, carpenters, cleaners, technicians, and more. You can browse by category, view profiles, and contact them directly on WhatsApp.

Are the pros vetted by Zaure?

Some are, some aren't. Zaure doesn't employ or vet every professional in the directory. We verify phone numbers and basic details, but we don't run background checks on every listing.

Before hiring: ask for references, confirm their qualifications, agree scope and price in writing (even a WhatsApp message counts), and never pay 100% upfront.
What if a pro does a bad job?

Zaure isn't a party to the contract between you and the pro. Disputes are between the two of you. But if a pro repeatedly gets reported for poor work or fraud, we will remove them from the directory.

Report bad experiences to us so we can protect other users.

Verification

What the verified badge means — and what it doesn't.

How do I get verified?

Message us on WhatsApp and request verification. For individual sellers, we check your phone and email. For business sellers, we additionally check CAC registration, physical address, and bank account details.

Verification is free. We never charge for the badge.

What does verified actually mean?

A verified badge means we've checked certain details at the time of verification — usually phone, email, and for businesses, CAC registration and address.

It does not mean:

  • That the seller will never scam you
  • That the item is exactly as described
  • That Zaure will refund you if things go wrong
Verification is one signal, not a guarantee. Always inspect, meet, and check before paying — verified or not.
Can someone lose their verified badge?

Yes. If we receive credible reports of fraud, misrepresentation, or policy violations, we'll revoke the badge and may suspend the account. Verification is a snapshot in time, not a permanent status.

Is verification data private?

Yes. Any documents you submit for verification (CAC, ID, address proof) are stored securely and used only to confirm your identity. They're never shown publicly — only the verified badge appears on your profile.

You can request deletion of verification documents at any time by contacting us. Your badge will be removed, and you'd need to re-verify to regain it.

WhatsApp & Communication

Why Zaure uses WhatsApp for all conversations.

Why doesn't Zaure have its own messaging?

Because Nigerian commerce already runs on WhatsApp. Buyers and sellers are already there — all day, for family, business, and everything in between. Asking users to check a second messaging app is asking them to change how they live, and most won't.

So we chose to build the discovery, trust, and verification layers — and hand off the conversation itself to WhatsApp, where it already works.

Can Zaure see my WhatsApp conversations?

No. Once you tap a WhatsApp button on Zaure and the conversation opens, that conversation is between you and the other party. Zaure cannot see, read, record, or access it in any way.

When you tap a WhatsApp button on Zaure, we log that a click happened (for analytics) — but not the content of the message you sent or the replies you got.

Should I share my personal info on WhatsApp?

Share only what's necessary for the transaction:

  • OK to share: your name, general area for meeting (e.g. "near Shoprite, Barnawa"), and payment confirmation.
  • Not OK: BVN, NIN, bank PIN, OTP codes, home address, copies of your ID with strangers.

If a "buyer" or "seller" asks for your BVN or OTP, they're a scammer. No legitimate transaction requires these.

Can I record or screenshot a conversation for evidence?

Yes — and we recommend it for high-value transactions. If you're spending above ₦100,000 or buying a vehicle, save screenshots of the key messages: price agreement, delivery terms, and payment confirmation.

If you report a seller or buyer to us, those screenshots are the evidence we'll act on.

Account

Manage your profile, preferences, and data.

How do I edit my profile?

Go to Profile → Edit Profile. Update your name, phone, email, state, or bio, then tap Save Changes.

How do I change my profile photo?

On your profile page, tap the camera icon on your avatar. Choose a photo from your device. Your photo is stored locally on your device — it won't sync across devices unless you're logged in on both.

How do I change the language or theme?

Language and dark mode settings are in Profile → Settings. Zaure supports English, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, and Arabic. Language preference applies site-wide.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Confirm twice. This permanently removes your profile, listings, and favorites.

If you also want server-side records deleted (beyond what the app removes immediately), message us on WhatsApp with your registered phone number — see our Privacy Policy for retention details.

I lost my data after clearing my browser. Can I get it back?

Most of Zaure's data (favorites, listings, avatar, theme) is stored in your browser's localStorage — not on our servers. If you clear your browser data, it's gone.

This is a known limitation that we're working on. For now, if you want to preserve your listings, keep a backup of the text/photos somewhere else too.

Scams & Safety

The patterns to watch for and how to report.

What are the most common scams on Zaure?

The same ones that have been used on Nigerian marketplaces for years:

  • "I'm out of town — I'll ship it." Seller wants advance payment before you see anything.
  • The fake alert. Buyer shows a fake payment screenshot — always check your actual bank app.
  • The overpayment refund. Buyer sends "too much" and asks you to refund the difference — their original transfer was fake.
  • The military/pastor emergency. Fake credentials to bypass caution.
  • The "agent" pickup. Someone collects on the buyer's behalf, then the buyer claims they never received.
  • The fake Zaure verification fee. Anyone claiming to be from Zaure asking you to pay for verification is a scammer. We never charge.
How do I report a scam or suspicious activity?

Message us on WhatsApp at +234 904 233 9411 with:

  • Screenshots of the conversation
  • The listing link (if there was one)
  • A short description of what happened

We act on all reports within 24 hours. If it's clear fraud, we'll suspend the account and share details with Nigerian authorities if you want to file a police report.

Can I recover money I lost to a scam?

Zaure can't recover money that was paid directly to a seller — we're not a payment processor or escrow service. Your options are:

  • Report to us — we'll suspend the seller's account so they can't scam others
  • Report to your bank — if you paid by transfer, file a fraud report. Some banks can attempt a reversal for very recent transfers.
  • Report to the police — for significant amounts, file a formal report. We'll cooperate with the investigation.
  • Report to EFCC — for large-scale fraud, the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission can act.
What is Zaure doing to prevent scams?

We're actively working on this. Currently we:

  • Verify seller details before granting a badge
  • Act on user reports within 24 hours
  • Suspend accounts with clear evidence of fraud
  • Publish these safety guides so users know the patterns

We're also planning: buyer verification (to stop fake orders), escrow for high-value transactions, and stronger seller identity checks. These take time and money — but they're on the roadmap.
