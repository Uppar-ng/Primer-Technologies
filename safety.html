Safety Tips

Stay safe while buying and selling on Zaure

Meet in Public Places

Always meet buyers or sellers in well-lit, public locations like shopping malls, police stations, or busy markets. Never invite strangers to your home.

Inspect Before Purchase

Thoroughly inspect any item before paying. Test electronics, check for damage, and verify authenticity. Don't rush the process.

Avoid Advance Payments

Never pay for items you haven't seen. Be cautious of sellers who demand advance payments or deposits before delivery.

Trust Your Instincts

If something feels too good to be true, it probably is. Trust your gut and walk away from suspicious deals.

Check Seller Verification

Look for the "Verified" badge on sellers. Verified sellers have been checked by Zaure for authenticity.

Keep Communication on Zaure

Use Zaure's contact buttons to reach sellers. Avoid sharing personal information unnecessarily.

Report Suspicious Activity

If you encounter a suspicious listing or seller, report it immediately.
